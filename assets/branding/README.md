# HGE emblem

The emblem comes from the left blue/green symbol in the
[HGE banner](https://www.ufrgs.br/hge/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/HGE-2.png)
published by UFRGS's Hidrologia de Grande Escala group. The original 940 × 200
PNG has SHA-256
`76420a30b6f1d9c1f175f4d0cb715770eb04dba1133f7e9a51758c92216a3b30`.

The icon isolates the 190 × 190 region beginning at pixel (97, 5), removes the
connected near-white backdrop, and adds transparent padding without redrawing
the symbol. The PNGs and multi-resolution ICO retain the original colors and
proportions. The HGE wordmark and partner logos are omitted.

The emblem identifies HGE; it is not covered by the Twemoji artwork license.
